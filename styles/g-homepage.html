01. The Formula

Every good prompt has 4 PIECES:

PIECEANSWERSIF MISSING
WHATgoal + exact locationexplores the whole repo
LIMITwhat not to touchscope creep
DONEproof it's finishednever stops
HOWmodel, agents, ask/decideinherits the big model
